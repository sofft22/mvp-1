Questions people ask

Do my customers have to sign up?

No. They open your link, look through your products, and message you or pay by transfer.

Do I need a website?

No. Your BIOSHOP link is your store. You paste it in your bio and share it anywhere.

How do I get paid?

Customers send money straight to the bank account you add to your store. BIOSHOP doesn't hold your money.

Why is there no cart?

Because your customers already order on WhatsApp. We kept that, and removed everything that gets in the way.

How does the launch offer work?

The first 20 sellers to upgrade get 50% off their first month. Starter is ₦2,500 and Pro is ₦5,000 for that month. After that, the normal price applies.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. The free plan lets you add 5 products with 1 image each. Upgrade when your shelf gets bigger.

What will my link look like?

Something like BIOSHOP.pages.dev/store/yourname, using the name you choose.
